## Rules from outside the videos

Labelled Convention. From the first test audit, where the most damaging problems had no source rule: GA1 (gates keep the user's place), SC1 (overlays), SC2 (every width and language), SC3 (everyone can reach the action), UN8 (what paying adds), SC6 (empty, loading and error states).

Added for the journey walk, because each hand-off needs a check of its own: JO1 (choices carry over), JO3 (attention carries over), PA1 (the charge matches the commitment), PA3 (a failed payment keeps the order), EX3 (a cancel route as short as the sign-up). EX3 turns honesty item HO3 into a check with a fix. JO4 (back never re-enters a finished step) came from the navigation rules in Appllama's app design skill (appllama-skills, reviewed 2026-10-30), rewritten for the browser.

Rejected source tips (fake progress, "I'll risk it", the complaint that "Maybe later" is too easy) appear in the tables above with the rule or guard that rejects them; the honesty items HO1 to HO13 in honesty.md apply the guard in SKILL.md. HO11 and HO12 turn two rejected tips into checkable items (fake progress; a paid item labelled as a personal match). HO13 (marketing consent bundled into a stock alert) came from the comparison audits of a store product page, where two independent runs found it and no rule covered it.
