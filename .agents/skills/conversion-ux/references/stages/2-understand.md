# Stage 2: Understand

The user is asking: what is it, is it for me, what do I get first, what does it cost and when, what does paying add, can I trust it?

Starts when the user reads for detail and ends when they make a first selection or reach the primary action. Hand-off rules and severity: [journey.md](../journey.md).

## Hand-off in and out

- **In**: the promise from Arrive (an ad's price, the plan name clicked) matches what this stage states.
- **Out**: write down every price, term, date and claim stated here. Commit and Pay must honour them (CO1, PA1); a later stage that changes one is a hand-off finding.

## Rules

### Applies when: always

**UN1. Hard question to easy question.** Find the question the headline and price put to someone who has not used the product ("Is this worth $19 a month?"). Fix: lead with one they can answer now: how the trial works, what they get first, what paying adds, what it costs today. zr37ibqXl1U 1:32-2:28, 14:00. Presenter.

**UN2. Numbers and finished arithmetic.** "Quick setup", "fast delivery", "Mar 28-Apr 2" leave work for the user. Fix: the measured number ("23 minutes"), day names and counts ("Fri, Mar 28 to Wed, Apr 2 · 5 nights"), totals and per-unit prices. zr37ibqXl1U 3:56, 13:06. Presenter. Guard: true for a typical user, not the best case.

**UN3. Keep only what helps the decision.** Every label, badge and stat is one more thing to read; elements meant for another audience (a "Manage" link shown to logged-out visitors) are noise. Fix: cut what does not change this user's choice. U7jTCgIWS4w 15:01; GGg61sdEjeI 9:44. Craft.

The next four are screen-craft rules and count toward the Polish cap.

**UN4. Hierarchy you can scan.** Text of nearly equal size, weight and colour forces reading. Fix: name up, key number (rating, price) with its own weight, the rest recedes. U7jTCgIWS4w 1:45, 2:29. Craft.

**UN5. Type.** Several families; titles that shout; dense paragraphs; cramped small caps. Fix: one family, hierarchy from size, weight, colour and line height; looser body line height; letter spacing on small uppercase. GGg61sdEjeI 4:53-6:51. Craft.

**UN6. Short badges, quiet support elements.** "20% off discount" plus icon; mixed icon styles; dark dividers; uneven gaps. Fix: badge text read in one glance; one icon style; soft dividers; spacing that groups related things. GGg61sdEjeI 5:17, 8:18-9:44. Craft.

**UN7. Redundant or misleading labels.** "Price:" before an amount; a title holding a value the user can change ("1 kg" beside a quantity picker). Fix: remove the label; move the value to its control (CO5). GGg61sdEjeI 9:44-10:39. Craft.

### Applies when: the screen shows a price, a plan or a fee

**UN8. Say what paying adds.** Where a free tier or free path exists, the page must state the difference plainly and early, in the user's terms: which features, which limits, how soon. Claims like "more of everything" must hold against the free tier. Convention.

**UN9. One number per option, with its unit.** Ranges make users price the worst case; "$3" alone leaves "per what?". Fix: one upfront price per option with its period ("$3/month", "$20/year"). zr37ibqXl1U 7:38. Presenter. Guard: only when the product honours that price; if it varies, show the likely price, why it varies and the cap. A custom-priced tier ("Contact sales") is legitimate; give a starting price or what sets the price when you can.

**UN10. Convenience beside cost.** A price alone is a cost decision. Fix: the time or effort it saves next to it ("2 minutes away"; "or about 6 weeks of earning points"), or the monthly equivalent of a large one-off price ("est. $4,450/mo"). zr37ibqXl1U 8:45. Presenter. Guard: true estimate from real data.

**UN11. Price and trust near the title.** Price far down the page; rating far from the name. Fix: name, rating and price together near the top. GGg61sdEjeI 6:51, 10:39. Craft.

**UN12. Reference prices.** A struck-through earlier price makes the current one read as a deal. zr37ibqXl1U 12:47. Adjacent research. Guard: a genuine earlier price for the same item. For goods sold to consumers in the EU, the earlier price shown with a reduction must be the lowest of the previous 30 days, subject to national rules and exceptions; that rule does not cover services or digital content, where the general principle still holds.

**UN13. Like-for-like context.** A fee alone feels large; beside what it protects it reads small. 2TlIg3VokY8 9:36. Adjacent research. Guard: same units. The video's "$49/month · just 2.6%" of a one-off $1,899 price is about 31% of it per year; show "$588 a year" instead. Recheck at Pay, where add-ons are often framed this way (PA2).

### Applies when: the screen asks for payment, an upgrade, a trial or an add-on

**UN14. Explain how the offer works before selling it.** With a trial: what unlocks today, the reminder, the charge day and amount, as a short timeline. Without a trial: what is charged today, when it renews, how to stop it. zr37ibqXl1U 2:05-2:50. Presenter. Guard: real dates and amounts; list a reminder only if the product sends one.

**UN15. Volunteer the downside.** State when they pay and how to stop before they ask. zr37ibqXl1U 2:51. Adjacent research ("transparency bias" is the video's label; see [evidence.md](../evidence.md)).

## Honesty items here

HO2 (urgency or scarcity), HO6 (reviews, ratings, counts), HO7 (mixed units, invented reference prices), HO8 (plan claims), HO9 (trial and renewal terms). Details in [honesty.md](../honesty.md).

## Build moves

- **List the user's questions in order.** What is this, is it for me, what do I get first, what does paying add, what does it cost and when, how much effort, can I undo it, can I trust it. For each, decide which element answers it and where (headline, beside the price, the line beside the button). A question with no answer on the screen is a reason to leave (UN1, UN8, CO2).
- **Make the hardest question smaller.** Find the one question you cannot avoid (usually money or commitment). Choose what makes it easier and true: a trial explained as a timeline, a free partial result, a preset, a guarantee, a cancellation window. Put that first (UN1, UN14, GA2).
- **Price honestly and completely.** One price per option with its unit; what paying adds over free, stated early; true reference prices only; same units when comparing (UN8, UN9, UN12, UN13). Mandatory fees go in the first price shown, not the last step (HO5).
