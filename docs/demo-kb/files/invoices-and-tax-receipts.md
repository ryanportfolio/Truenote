# Invoices and Tax Receipts

Cloudshelf Customer Support · Reference CS-REF-025 · Version 2 · Effective September 1, 2026 · Owner: Billing Operations

## When to use this

Use this reference when a customer needs a copy of an invoice or receipt, wants a company name, address or tax ID on their invoices, or asks about sales tax on their charges.

## Where invoices live

Every charge creates an invoice. The customer gets an email receipt right away, and every invoice from the past 7 years is under Billing settings, then Invoices, as a PDF. Refunds create a credit note, also emailed immediately.

## Steps to help a customer find an invoice

1. Ask the customer to open Billing settings, then Invoices.
2. Have them select the invoice date and choose Download PDF.
3. If they cannot sign in, verify the caller with two factors and select Resend invoice in the billing console. It goes only to the account email.
4. Add an account note with the invoice date you resent.

Agents never email invoices to an address that is not on the account.

## Changing invoice details

| Detail | Who can change it | Applies to |
|---|---|---|
| Company name and billing address | Account owner, in Billing settings | Future invoices, plus reissued past invoices |
| Tax ID (VAT, GST or EIN) | Account owner, in Billing settings | Future invoices only |
| Purchase order number | Enterprise account administrator | Future invoices only |
| Invoice billing by bank transfer | Billing Operations, Enterprise annual plans only | From the next renewal |

To reissue a past invoice with a new company name or address, the customer selects Reissue on that invoice after updating Billing settings. Past invoices can be reissued for up to 12 months back. Tax IDs are never added to past invoices; that request goes to Billing Operations as a ticket.

## Sales tax

Prices are in dollars and exclude sales tax. Tax is added at checkout based on the billing address of the card on file, and each invoice shows the tax as its own line. Agents cannot remove tax from a charge.

Tax-exempt organizations can send an exemption certificate to Billing Operations through a ticket. Once approved, future charges are tax-free. Past tax is not refunded.

## Common questions

**"My invoice shows a different amount from the plan price."** Check for sales tax, a prorated upgrade charge, or added Enterprise seats. Each appears as its own line.

**"I need a receipt for a refund."** The credit note was emailed when the refund was submitted. It is also under Invoices. The refund itself posts to the original card within 5-7 business days.

**"Can I get one invoice for the whole year?"** Annual plans already bill once at the start of the term. Monthly customers can download a yearly statement from Invoices, then Statements.

**"Can you change the date on an invoice?"** No. Invoice dates match the charge date.
