# Storage Limits and Overage

Larkspur Cloud Customer Support · Reference CS-REF-018 · Version 2 · Effective September 1, 2026 · Owner: Product Marketing

## When to use this

Use this reference when a customer says uploads stopped working, received a storage warning email, asks how much space their plan includes, or wants to know what happens if they go over their limit.

## Storage by plan

| Plan | Storage | Version history | Trash kept for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Basic | 200 GB | 30 days | 30 days |
| Pro | 2 TB shared by up to 5 users | 90 days | 30 days |
| Enterprise | 5 TB per user | 1 year | 30 days |

Files in Trash and older file versions count toward storage. Emptying Trash frees space right away.

## There are no overage charges

Larkspur Cloud never bills for storage above the plan limit and does not sell add-on storage packs. When an account reaches its limit, uploads pause instead. The only way to get more space is to delete files or upgrade to a larger plan.

## What the customer sees

| Usage | What happens |
|---|---|
| 90% of the limit | Warning email and a banner in the app |
| 100% of the limit | New uploads and sync pause; existing files stay available to view, download and share |
| Over the limit after a downgrade | Account becomes read-only on the renewal date until usage is back under the limit |

## Steps to help a customer who is out of space

1. Ask the customer to open Settings, then Storage, and read you the usage figure.
2. Suggest emptying Trash first. Files there are deleted for good after 30 days anyway.
3. Point them to the largest files list on the Storage page.
4. Explain that older versions of edited files count toward storage. On Pro and Enterprise, the account owner can remove old versions from the file's Version history panel.
5. If they still need more room, explain the upgrade options and pricing (see Plan Upgrades and Proration). Upgrades take effect immediately and the extra storage is available as soon as the payment clears.
6. Add an account note with the usage figure and what the customer chose.

You do not need to verify the caller to explain plan limits. Verify with two factors before you look at or discuss the customer's own usage.

## Downgrades and storage

Downgrades take effect at the next renewal. If usage is above the lower plan's limit on that date, the account becomes read-only until the customer deletes files or upgrades again. Always check usage before scheduling a downgrade and tell the customer the number they need to get under. Example: a Pro customer using 450 GB who downgrades to Basic must get below 200 GB before the renewal date.

## Common questions

**"Will you delete my files if I'm over?"** No. Files are never deleted because an account is over its limit.

**"Can I buy 100 GB extra?"** No add-on storage exists. Offer the next plan up.

**"My Pro team is out of space but I only use a little."** Pro storage is shared by all users on the plan. The account owner can see each user's share on the Storage page.
