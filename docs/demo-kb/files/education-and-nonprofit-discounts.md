# Education and Nonprofit Discounts

Larkspur Cloud Customer Support · Policy CS-POL-027 · Version 2 · Effective September 1, 2026 · Owner: Billing Operations

## When to use this

Use this policy when a customer asks about a student, teacher, school or nonprofit discount, or asks whether any other discount exists.

## The discount

Verified nonprofits and education customers get 30% off Pro and Enterprise. Billing Operations approves every discount. Basic is not eligible. This is the only discount Larkspur Cloud offers; agents may not create or promise any other discount.

## Who qualifies

| Customer | Eligible plans | Proof Billing Operations accepts |
|---|---|---|
| Registered nonprofit | Pro, Enterprise | Registration document or tax-exempt determination letter |
| School, college or university | Pro, Enterprise | Accreditation record, or an invoice request on school letterhead |
| Student enrolled at an accredited school | Pro | Current enrollment letter or student ID showing this term |
| Teacher or faculty member | Pro | Staff ID or a letter from the school |
| Government agency | Not eligible | Not applicable |
| Business that donates to charity | Not eligible | Not applicable |

## Steps

1. You do not need to verify the caller to explain the discount. Verify with two factors before you open a request on an account.
2. Confirm the customer is on Pro or Enterprise, or is about to upgrade. Enterprise requests need the account administrator.
3. Open a ticket for Billing Operations and attach nothing yourself. Billing Operations emails the customer a secure upload link for the proof.
4. Tell the customer Billing Operations reviews requests within 2 business days.
5. Once approved, the discount starts on the next charge. It is not applied to past charges.
6. Add an account note with the ticket number.

## Rules

- The discount does not stack with anything else and cannot be added on top of an outage or courtesy credit.
- Student and teacher discounts are reviewed every 12 months. The customer gets an email 30 days before the review asking for new proof; without it, the plan returns to the standard price at the next renewal.
- Nonprofit and school discounts are reviewed every 2 years.
- A discount is attached to the account, not to a person. It cannot be moved to a different account.

## Example prices with the discount

| Plan | Standard price | With 30% off |
|---|---|---|
| Pro monthly | $11.99 | $8.39 |
| Pro annual | $119 per year | $83.30 per year |
| Enterprise | $24 per user a month | $16.80 per user a month |

## What to say if the customer does not qualify

"That discount is only for verified schools, students, teachers and nonprofits. I'm not able to offer a different discount, but I can walk you through the plan options to find the best fit."

If the customer asks for a supervisor, escalate to a Tier 2 supervisor within 15 minutes. Supervisors cannot create discounts either.
