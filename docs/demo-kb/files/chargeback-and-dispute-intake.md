# Chargeback and Dispute Intake

Cloudshelf Customer Support · Procedure CS-PRO-024 · Version 3 · Effective September 1, 2026 · Owner: Billing Operations

## When to use this

Use this procedure when a customer says they disputed a Cloudshelf charge with their bank, a bank or card network sends a chargeback notice, or a customer wants to dispute a charge and has not yet called their bank.

Billing Operations owns every chargeback. A Tier 1 agent takes the details, opens the ticket and sets expectations. Agents do not decide chargebacks.

## Dispute or chargeback?

| Term | Meaning | Who handles it |
|---|---|---|
| Billing dispute | The customer disagrees with a charge and contacts us first | Tier 1 agent; escalate amount or window disputes to a Tier 2 supervisor |
| Chargeback | The customer's bank has reversed or is reversing the charge | Billing Operations, ticket queue BILL-CB, 1 business day |
| Duplicate charge | Two charges for the same thing | Refund with reason code RF-02 and open a ticket in queue BILL-DUP |

## Steps for a billing dispute (no chargeback yet)

1. Verify the caller with two factors.
2. Find the charge in the billing console and read the date, description and amount.
3. If the charge is within the 30-day refund window and qualifies under the Refund Procedure, offer the refund. It posts to the original card within 5-7 business days.
4. If the customer disputes the amount or the refund window, escalate to a Tier 2 supervisor within 15 minutes.
5. Ask the customer not to open a chargeback while the refund is pending; a refund and a chargeback on the same charge take longer to untangle.

## Steps for a chargeback

1. Verify the caller with two factors.
2. Do not issue a refund for a charge that is already in a chargeback. The bank has already returned the money; a refund would pay it twice.
3. Open a ticket in queue BILL-CB with the charge date, amount, the bank's reason if the customer knows it, and what the customer told you.
4. Tell the customer Billing Operations will respond within 1 business day, and that the bank makes the final decision.
5. Add an account note with the ticket number.

## What changes while a chargeback is open

- The customer cannot cancel the plan until Billing Operations closes the chargeback. Escalate cancellation requests to Billing Operations.
- Account deletion waits until the chargeback is closed (Privacy Requests guide).
- Do not change the plan, add seats or apply credits.
- The account stays usable unless Billing Operations decides otherwise.

## Script

"I can see the charge has been disputed with your bank. Our Billing Operations team handles that directly with the bank. I've opened a ticket for you, and they'll be in touch within 1 business day. While the dispute is open I'm not able to cancel or change the plan, but your files stay available."

## Never do this

- Never promise that Cloudshelf will accept or drop a chargeback.
- Never ask the customer to withdraw a chargeback in exchange for a refund or credit.
- Never take card details to "check" the disputed charge.
- Never open a second ticket for the same chargeback; add notes to the existing one.
