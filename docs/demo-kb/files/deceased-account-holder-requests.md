# Deceased Account Holder Requests

Larkspur Cloud Customer Support · Procedure CS-PRO-029 · Version 2 · Effective August 15, 2026 · Owner: Legal and Privacy

## When to use this

Use this procedure when a family member, executor or friend calls to tell us that a Larkspur Cloud account holder has died, and asks to close the account, stop charges, or get access to the files.

## How to speak with the caller

Start with "I'm very sorry for your loss." Keep the call simple and do not ask the caller to explain how the person died. These calls take as long as they take; they are not counted against handle time.

The caller cannot be verified as the account holder, so do not read out account details, files, charges or the card on file. You may confirm only what the caller already tells you.

## What the caller can ask for

| Request | Who can ask | What we need | Who handles it |
|---|---|---|---|
| Stop future charges | Anyone who reports the death | Account email and the account holder's name | Tier 1 agent pauses billing the same day |
| Close the account | Next of kin or executor | Death certificate | Legal and Privacy, queue LP-PRIV, 2 business days |
| Refund of a recent charge | Next of kin or executor | Death certificate | Tier 2 supervisor approval, reason code RF-05 |
| Access to or copy of files | Executor or court-appointed representative only | Death certificate plus a court document naming them | Legal and Privacy |
| Keep the account open and pay for it | Next of kin or executor | Death certificate | Legal and Privacy |

There is no cancellation fee when an account is closed because the holder has died.

## Steps

1. Express condolences and ask for the account holder's name and the email address on the account.
2. Find the account. Do not confirm details beyond what the caller has given you.
3. In the billing console, select Billing, then Pause billing, reason "Bereavement". This stops future charges right away and keeps the files untouched.
4. Ask for the caller's name, relationship and an email address for follow-up.
5. Open a ticket in queue LP-PRIV with that information and the request.
6. Tell the caller that Legal and Privacy will email them within 2 business days with a secure link to upload the death certificate and any court documents. Agents never collect documents by email or phone.
7. Add an account note: "Bereavement report, billing paused, LP-PRIV ticket (number)."

## Enterprise accounts

If the person who died was an ordinary member of an Enterprise organization, the organization's account administrator manages the seat and the files; give the caller that guidance and do not open a ticket. If the person was the only account administrator, open the LP-PRIV ticket so Legal and Privacy can work with the organization to name a new administrator.

## Files and privacy

Files are never deleted during a bereavement case. The account stays paused until Legal and Privacy closes it or hands it over. If the executor asks for deletion, the normal deletion stages apply once Legal and Privacy approves: soft delete for 30 days, permanent on day 31.

Never give anyone the password or sign them in to the account. Approved representatives receive a data export instead.

## If you are unsure

If the caller seems unsure the person has died, or you suspect the report is false (for example, the account holder signed in today), do not pause billing. Escalate to a Tier 2 supervisor within 15 minutes.
