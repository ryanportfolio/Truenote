CLOUDSHELF CUSTOMER SUPPORT
CALL SCRIPTS
Reference CS-SCR-001, Version 7, effective September 1, 2026. Owner: Support Operations.

Read the required lines word for word. The other lines are guidance; use your own words.

1. OPENING (required)
"Thank you for calling Cloudshelf, this is (your first name). This call may be recorded for quality and training. Can I start with the email address on your account?"

2. IDENTITY VERIFICATION
Verify two factors before discussing or changing anything on the account. See the Identity Verification procedure for the accepted factors.
If verification fails twice, say (required):
"I'm not able to verify the account over the phone right now. You can recover access from the sign-in page using 'Forgot password', or reply to the email we just sent."
Then end account-specific discussion. You may still answer general questions.

3. PLACING A CALL ON HOLD
Always ask first: "May I place you on a brief hold while I check that?"
Wait for a yes. Check back at least every 2 minutes. Never leave a caller on hold for more than 6 minutes in total; offer a callback instead.

4. TRANSFERRING TO A SUPERVISOR
"I'm going to bring in my supervisor, who can help with this. I'll stay on the line and explain what we've covered so you don't have to repeat it."
Use a warm transfer. Escalate within 15 minutes when the customer asks for a supervisor.

5. CARD DETAILS
Never ask a customer to read a full card number or security code aloud. If they start to, interrupt politely:
"Please don't read your card number to me. You can update it safely in Billing settings, and I'll stay on the line while you do."

6. CLOSING (required)
"Is there anything else I can help you with today?"
If no: "Thank you for choosing Cloudshelf. You'll get a short survey by email about this call. Have a good day."

7. AFTER THE CALL
Add a note to the account within 5 minutes of hanging up: reason for the call, what you did, and any follow-up promised with its date.
