# Enterprise Seat Management

Cloudshelf Customer Support · Procedure CS-PRO-016 · Version 2 · Effective September 1, 2026 · Owner: Billing Operations

## When to use this

Use this procedure when an Enterprise customer wants to add or remove users (seats), asks about the 5-user minimum, or a team member asks for access to their company's Enterprise account.

## Who can make seat changes

Only the account administrator can authorize seat changes, billing changes, cancellation or deletion for an Enterprise organization. An ordinary team member can be verified for their own user settings only. If a team member asks for more seats or for someone else's access, tell them to contact their administrator. Do not add, remove or reassign a seat at a team member's request.

Most administrators manage seats themselves in the admin console. Help over the phone only when the administrator asks for it.

## Steps to add seats

1. Verify the caller as the account administrator with two factors.
2. Confirm the number of seats to add and whether the plan is monthly or annual.
3. Open the organization in the billing console and select Seats, then Add seats.
4. Read the prorated charge shown in the console and the new monthly or annual total. Get a clear yes.
5. Submit. New seats are available as soon as the payment clears; the administrator then invites users from the admin console.
6. Add an account note with the old and new seat counts and the prorated charge.

## Steps to remove seats

1. Verify the caller as the account administrator with two factors.
2. Check that the new total stays at 5 users or more. The console will not accept fewer than 5.
3. Select Seats, then Remove seats, and choose how many.
4. Tell the administrator the change takes effect at the next renewal date, with no partial refund for the current period.
5. Remind them to move or download the departing users' files before the renewal date. Files in a removed seat are transferred to the administrator's account for 30 days, then deleted.

## Reassigning a seat

When an employee leaves, the administrator can reassign the seat to a new person at no charge in the admin console under Users. The departing user's files move to the administrator. Agents cannot reassign seats; walk the administrator through the console if asked.

## Single sign-on

Enterprise organizations can require single sign-on (SSO). When SSO is required, users cannot reset their Cloudshelf password, because sign-in goes through the company's identity provider. Send those users to their own IT team.

## Escalations

- A dispute about seat charges or a seat count goes to a Tier 2 supervisor within 15 minutes.
- A request for a discount on seats for a nonprofit or school goes to Billing Operations with proof of status (2 business days).
- If the person calling cannot be verified as the administrator but insists on changing seats, do not make the change. If you suspect an account takeover, lock the account and escalate to Security Response within 1 hour.
