# Retention Offers and the Save-the-Sale Script

Larkspur Cloud Customer Support · Script CS-SCR-006 · Version 3 · Effective September 1, 2026 · Owner: Support Operations

## When to use this

Use this script when a customer calls to cancel and has not yet confirmed the cancellation. The goal is to solve the reason behind the call, not to keep every customer at any cost. If the customer still wants to cancel after one offer, cancel for them.

## Ground rules

- Make one offer, the one that fits the customer's reason. Do not run through the whole list.
- Never refuse, delay or make cancelling harder. If the customer says "just cancel it", go straight to the cancellation steps in the Cancellation Policy.
- Agents may not create or promise discounts. None of the offers below is a discount.
- Customers in California and New York, and legacy customers who signed up before January 1, 2022, pay no cancellation fee. Never use the fee as pressure.
- A customer whose account is in an active chargeback cannot cancel until Billing Operations closes the chargeback. Escalate those calls to Billing Operations instead of making an offer.

## Steps

1. Verify the caller with two factors. Enterprise cancellations need the account administrator.
2. Ask the reason: "Before I process that, may I ask what's prompting the cancellation?"
3. Match the reason to one offer in the table below and make that offer once.
4. If the customer accepts, make the change and add an account note with the offer code.
5. If the customer declines, say "Understood, I'll take care of the cancellation now" and follow the Cancellation Policy steps.
6. Read the fee that applies (Basic $5, Pro $10, Enterprise $25 after the first 30 days, unless an exemption applies) and confirm the plan stays active until the end of the billing period.

## Offers by reason

| Reason | Offer | Code | Who approves |
|---|---|---|---|
| Too expensive | Downgrade to a lower plan at the next renewal | RT-DG | None |
| Too expensive, on Pro monthly | Switch to Pro annual at the published $119 a year | RT-AN | None |
| Not using it right now | Pause the subscription for 1, 2 or 3 months | RT-PA | None |
| Running out of space | Explain storage clean-up, or upgrade | RT-ST | None |
| Had an outage | Enterprise: outage credit under the uptime commitment. Basic or Pro: courtesy credit up to $5 | RT-OC | Tier 2 supervisor for courtesy credits |
| Moving to another service | No offer. Help with the data export | None | None |

## Pausing a subscription

A pause is available on Basic and Pro, once in any 12 months, for 1 to 3 months. During a pause there is no charge, files stay available to view and download, and uploads are turned off. The plan resumes on its own at the end of the pause and the customer is charged the normal price. Enterprise plans cannot be paused.

## Script lines

Opening the offer: "Thanks for telling me. One option that might help is (offer). Would you like me to set that up, or would you prefer to go ahead with the cancellation?"

If they decline: "No problem at all. I'll cancel it now. Your plan stays active until (end of billing period), and you'll get a confirmation email within 24 hours."

Do not repeat the offer, ask "are you sure?" more than once, or transfer the customer to another team to hear more offers.

## After the call

Add an account note within 5 minutes: reason given, offer made with its code, and the outcome (accepted, declined, cancelled).
