# Plan Upgrades and Proration

Larkspur Cloud Customer Support · Procedure CS-PRO-014 · Version 2 · Effective September 1, 2026 · Owner: Billing Operations

## When to use this

Use this guide when a customer wants to move to a higher plan, asks why their upgrade charge is less than the full plan price, or wants to switch between monthly and annual billing. For downgrades, see "Downgrades" below and the Plans and Pricing Reference.

## How upgrades work

Upgrades take effect immediately. The customer pays a prorated charge for the rest of the current billing period, calculated by day. Storage and user limits increase as soon as the payment clears. The next full charge happens on the existing renewal date at the new plan's price.

## Steps to upgrade a customer

1. Verify the caller with two factors. Enterprise upgrades need the account administrator.
2. Open the account in the billing console and select Change plan.
3. Choose the new plan and billing term. The console shows the prorated amount before you submit.
4. Read the prorated amount and the next renewal date to the customer and get a clear yes.
5. Submit the change. The customer gets an email receipt for the prorated charge right away.
6. Add an account note with the old plan, the new plan and the prorated amount.

If the prorated charge is declined, the plan does not change. Help the customer update the card (see Updating a Payment Method) and try again.

## Proration examples

The console does the math. These examples help you explain it.

| Change | Situation | Prorated charge |
|---|---|---|
| Basic monthly to Pro monthly | 15 days left in a 30-day period | ($11.99 minus $4.99) x 15/30 = $3.50 |
| Pro monthly to Enterprise monthly, 5 users | 10 days left in a 30-day period | ($120.00 minus $11.99) x 10/30 = $36.00 |
| Basic annual to Pro annual | 6 months left in the term | ($119 minus $49) x 6/12 = $35.00 |

Enterprise is $24 per user a month with a 5-user minimum, so the smallest Enterprise plan is $120 a month.

## Monthly and annual billing

- Monthly to annual: takes effect immediately. Unused days of the current month are credited against the annual charge.
- Annual to monthly: takes effect at the end of the annual term. There is no refund for the rest of the annual term.

Annual Pro costs $119 a year, compared with $143.88 for twelve months at $11.99. You may point out that difference; it is the published price, not a discount.

## Downgrades

Downgrades take effect at the next renewal date. There is no partial refund for the current period. If the customer's stored data is above the lower plan's storage limit on the renewal date, the account becomes read-only until they delete files or upgrade again. Warn the customer about this before you schedule the downgrade.

## Common questions

**"Why was I charged twice this month?"** One charge is the prorated upgrade and one is the regular renewal. Show the customer both lines on the invoice. If the amounts do not match the console, open a Billing Operations ticket in queue BILL-DUP.

**"Can I upgrade for one month and go back?"** Yes. The upgrade is immediate and prorated; the downgrade waits for the next renewal.

**"Can you give me the upgrade for free?"** No. Agents may not create or promise discounts.
