# Outages and Service Credits: Agent FAQ

Cloudshelf Customer Support · FAQ CS-FAQ-012 · Version 4 · Effective September 1, 2026 · Owner: Support Operations

## How do I check whether there is an outage?

Open the status page at status.cloudshelf.example. If it shows an active incident, read the customer the latest public update word for word. Do not guess at causes or fix times.

If the status page shows no incident but three or more customers report the same problem within 30 minutes, post in the incident channel. The Incident Commander decides whether to declare an outage.

## Which customers get service credits?

Only Enterprise customers have an uptime commitment: 99.9% per calendar month.

| Monthly uptime | Credit on the next invoice |
|---|---|
| 99.0% to below 99.9% | 10% of the monthly fee |
| Below 99.0% | 25% of the monthly fee |

Basic and Pro plans have no uptime commitment and no automatic credit. A Tier 2 supervisor may approve a courtesy credit of up to $5.

## How does an Enterprise customer claim a credit?

The account administrator must request it within 30 days after the end of the month in which the outage happened. Submit the request in the billing console under Credits > Outage credit, not through Issue Refund. Billing Operations confirms the uptime figure and applies the credit to the next invoice. Credits are never paid out as cash refunds or to a card.

## What if the customer wants to cancel because of an outage?

Follow the Cancellation Policy. The outage does not waive the cancellation fee unless a Tier 2 supervisor approves a courtesy refund.

## Is the outage line different from the support line?

Yes. The outage line, extension 4400, stays open 24/7, including holidays when regular support is closed.
