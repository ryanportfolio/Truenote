Payment Decline Codes: What to Tell the Customer

Cloudshelf Billing Operations · Quick reference QR-BIL-07 · Updated September 2026

CodeMeaningWhat to tell the customerRetry?
D01Insufficient funds"Your bank declined the payment. You can try another card or try again later."Customer may retry
D05Do not honor"Your bank declined the payment without a reason. Please call the number on the back of your card."After the customer calls the bank
D14Invalid card number"The card number did not go through. Please re-enter it in Billing settings."Yes, after re-entry
D41Card reported lost or stolen"This card can't be used. Please add a different card." Do not mention lost or stolen.Never retry this card
D54Expired card"The card on file has expired. Please update the expiry date or add a new card."Yes, after update
D91Card issuer unavailable"Your bank's system was temporarily unavailable. We'll try again automatically."Automatic retry after 1 hour
Renewal grace period: after a failed renewal, the system retries 3 times over 7 days. If all retries fail, the account becomes read-only. Files are never deleted for a failed payment alone.
Never ask the customer to read a full card number or security code aloud. Card details are entered only by the customer in Billing settings.
