Changing plans

Upgrades

Upgrades take effect immediately. The customer pays a prorated charge for the rest of the current billing period, calculated by day. Storage and user limits increase as soon as the payment clears.

Downgrades

Downgrades take effect at the next renewal date. There is no partial refund for the current period. If the customer's stored data exceeds the lower plan's storage limit on the renewal date, the account becomes read-only until they delete files or upgrade again.

Switching between monthly and annual billing

Monthly to annual: takes effect immediately; unused days of the current month are credited against the annual charge. Annual to monthly: takes effect at the end of the annual term.
