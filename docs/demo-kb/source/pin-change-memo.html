Account PIN length changes from 4 to 6 digits

Effective October 1, 2026, every account PIN used for phone verification must have 6 digits. Four-digit PINs stop working on that date.

What agents must do on calls from October 1:

  1. If a caller gives a 4-digit PIN, do not accept it as a verification factor. Use two other factors instead.
  2. Tell the caller to set a new 6-digit PIN at their next sign-in under Security settings.
  3. Agents cannot set or read out a PIN for the customer.

Questions go to your Tier 2 supervisor.
