Cancellation Policy

Policy ID CS-POL-003 · Version 5
Effective September 1, 2026
Owner: Billing Operations

Policy

Customers may cancel any subscription plan within 30 days of purchase for a full refund. After 30 days, the standard cancellation fee for the customer's plan applies, and the plan stays active until the end of the current billing period.

Standard cancellation fees

PlanCancellation fee (after 30 days)Access after cancellation
Basic$5Until the end of the billing period
Pro$10Until the end of the billing period
Enterprise$25Until the end of the billing period; admin must confirm

Exceptions

  • Legacy customers who signed up before January 1, 2022 are exempt from cancellation fees on any plan.
  • Customers who cancel within 14 days of a price-increase notice pay no cancellation fee.
  • Customers whose account is in an active chargeback cannot cancel until Billing Operations closes the chargeback. Escalate these calls to Billing Operations.
If more than one rule applies, the one that results in no fee wins. Never charge a fee to a legacy customer or to a customer who cancels within 14 days of a price-increase notice.

How to cancel

  1. Open the account settings page.
  2. Select Cancel Subscription.
  3. Confirm the cancellation reason.
  4. The customer receives a confirmation email within 24 hours.

Agents may cancel on the customer's behalf only after identity verification (see the Identity Verification procedure). Enterprise cancellations require the account administrator, not an ordinary team member.

Data after cancellation

Files stay available for download for 30 days after the plan ends. After that, the account enters the deletion process described in the Privacy Requests guide.
