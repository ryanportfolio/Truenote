Privacy Requests: Data Export and Account Deletion

Guide CS-GDE-021 · Version 2
Effective August 15, 2026
Owner: Legal and Privacy

Data export

Any verified account owner can request a full export of their files and account data.

  1. Verify the caller's identity using two factors (Identity Verification procedure).
  2. In the admin tools, open Privacy > Export Request and submit the request.
  3. Tell the customer the export link arrives by email within 30 days, usually within 3 business days. The link expires 7 days after it is sent.

Account deletion

Deletion is permanent and cannot be reversed after the waiting period.

StageWhat happensCan the customer undo it?
Request submittedAccount is locked; sharing links stop workingYes
Days 1 to 30Soft delete; data is kept but inaccessibleYes, by contacting support
Day 31Permanent deletion of files and account dataNo

When deletion must wait

  • An open chargeback or billing dispute: deletion waits until Billing Operations closes it.
  • A legal hold: never discuss or confirm a legal hold with the customer. Escalate to Legal and Privacy.
  • Enterprise accounts: only the account administrator can request deletion of the organization.
Requests from police, courts or lawyers (subpoenas, warrants, preservation letters) are never handled by support. Do not confirm whether an account exists. Give the caller the legal requests address, legal@larkspur.example, and escalate to Legal and Privacy.
